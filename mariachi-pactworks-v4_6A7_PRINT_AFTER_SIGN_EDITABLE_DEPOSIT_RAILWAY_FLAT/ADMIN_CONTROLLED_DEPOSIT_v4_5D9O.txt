PactWorks v4.5D9O

DEPOSIT CONTROL CORRECTION

- $100 remains available as an ADMIN deposit preset.
- The customer does NOT always see a permanent $100 payment choice.
- The customer sees the required deposit amount that the admin set for that booking.
- Example:
  Admin sets deposit = $100 -> customer sees Pay required deposit $100.
  Admin sets deposit = $200 -> customer sees Pay required deposit $200.
  Admin sets another amount -> customer sees that amount.
- Pay in full remains available.
- Existing custom-payment control remains bounded by the required deposit.
- Zelle-only customer payment flow remains unchanged.
- No database changes.
