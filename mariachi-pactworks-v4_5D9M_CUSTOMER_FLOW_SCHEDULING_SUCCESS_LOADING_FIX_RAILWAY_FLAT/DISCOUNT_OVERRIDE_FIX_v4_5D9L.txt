PactWorks v4.5D9L

DISCOUNT INPUT FIX

Problem:
- The booking form's Campaign value field was readonly.
- A sync function existed but the field had no input/change listener.

Fix:
- Campaign value is now editable.
- Selecting a named discount loads that program's saved default.
- Admin may change the amount/percentage for the current quote.
- The quote recalculates immediately while typing.
- Percentage overrides are capped at 100%.
- The quote-level override does NOT rewrite the saved master discount program.
- Clearing the discount program disables the manual discount logically and resets the quote value.

No database changes.
