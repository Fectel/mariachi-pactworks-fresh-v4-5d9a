PRICING / MINIMUM RULE CORRECTION

The 2-hour evening minimum now applies ONLY:
- Friday
- Saturday
- for starts in the configured 5–8 PM evening window

Sunday is NOT part of the weekend 2-hour minimum.
Sunday follows the normal local minimum unless another rule independently
requires a longer booking, such as travel-distance minimums.

- duplicated public-booking copies
