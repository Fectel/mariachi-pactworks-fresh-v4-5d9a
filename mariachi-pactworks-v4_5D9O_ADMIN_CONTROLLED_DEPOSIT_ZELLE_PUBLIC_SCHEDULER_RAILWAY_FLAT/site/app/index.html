What is a locked backup?
  • It is one file containing your complete PactWorks database.
  • The file is intentionally unreadable by itself; that protects client details and signatures.
  • To use it, open PactWorks, choose Restore, and enter the same vault password.
  • Keep copies on two devices, such as your computer and a private USB drive.
